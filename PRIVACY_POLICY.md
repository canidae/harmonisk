# Privacy Policy for Harmonisk

**Last updated:** 2026-10-11

This Privacy Policy describes how **Harmonisk** (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information. The App is provided by **Vidar Wahlberg** (“Developer”).

## 1. Information We Collect

Harmonisk does **not** collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal information or user data.

We do not collect:

- Personal identification information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, or similar identifiers
- Location data
- Usage data, analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data
- Photos, contacts, files, microphone data, camera data, or other media
- Any other personal or sensitive user information

## 2. How We Use Information

Because Harmonisk does not collect any information, we do not use, process, or disclose user data for any purpose.

## 3. Local Storage

Harmonisk does not store user data on external servers.

If the App saves settings, preferences, or similar information, that information is stored **only locally on your device** and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. You can remove locally stored information by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling the App.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Harmonisk does not use third-party services that collect user data, including analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade user information with third parties, because we do not collect any user information.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Harmonisk does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected, the App does not knowingly collect or use information from children.

## 7. Security

Since Harmonisk does not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no personal data held by us that could be subject to a data breach.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
